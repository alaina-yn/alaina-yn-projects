What these tools do—and do not do

Each simulation presents its exact scoring and decision rules in the result. No data is transmitted or persisted automatically. JSON export downloads the inputs, derived result and a note that all data is synthetic. Reviewers remain accountable for actual decisions; thresholds are not normative framework prescriptions.
